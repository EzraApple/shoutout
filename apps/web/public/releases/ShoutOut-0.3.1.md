# ShoutOut 0.3.1

- Starts repeat recordings faster while keeping the microphone off between recordings.
- Lets you drag Glass and Classic indicators to any screen edge, and the crab to the left or right edge.
- Remembers indicator placement for each style and improves hover and drag handling.
- Adds a choice to leave system audio unchanged or dim it to 10% or less during recording.
- Restores system volume reliably after short recordings.
- Cancels an active recording safely when the microphone changes or the Mac sleeps.
